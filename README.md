Rules:
Start: Two teams, $500 each. Join by typing your team’s check-in code on your phone.

Every turn: Everyone on your team answers a trivia question.
75% of your team must get it right for the team to move forward.
Right: move forward and collect cash. The faster you answer, the more you earn.
Wrong: You get whatever the game gives you…

Special rule: the House Offer
When you land on a house, pick one:

Play it safe: take $250 and move 3 spaces.
Go double: risk it for double the money. If it fails, you stay put and lose $100.

Winning: The team with the most money at the end wins.
